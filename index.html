DESIGNED WITH PURPOSE

Better job searching, by design.

This educational prototype responds to UX issues identified in a SEEK website analysis: unclear salary and work-type information, crowded filters, and difficulty comparing jobs. It prioritises job seekers while helping employers present clearer vacancies. No real applications or personal data are collected.
